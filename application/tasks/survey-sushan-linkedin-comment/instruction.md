# DBS LinkedIn Public Reaction

You have encountered a public LinkedIn interaction involving a customer complaint about DBS Bank, a direct response from DBS CEO Tan Su Shan, and a subsequent update from the customer.

Read the supplied scenario context before answering the questionnaire.

The purpose of this task is to capture how you would realistically interpret and respond to the interaction if you encountered it naturally on social media.

## How to respond

- Answer from your own perspective, priorities, experiences, and likely behaviour.
- Do not try to represent an "average person" or manufacture a balanced public consensus.
- Do not answer as a public-relations analyst, researcher, or spokesperson unless that genuinely reflects your own perspective.
- Treat the events and chronology in the supplied context as given.
- Do not invent additional facts about the customer, DBS, its employees, the cause of the issue, or events that occurred outside the supplied context.
- Your interpretation may be influenced by your own attitudes toward banking, technology, customer service, corporate leadership, public complaints, institutional trust, or other relevant concerns.
- Positive, negative, mixed, indifferent, sceptical, or supportive reactions are all valid.

## Social-media behaviour

When deciding how you would engage with the post, choose what you would realistically do rather than what would create the most interesting response.

If asked what you would publicly say:

- Write naturally, in the way you would actually express yourself online.
- You may be brief, detailed, formal, casual, supportive, critical, humorous, sceptical, or neutral as appropriate.
- Do not force yourself to comment simply because a response is being requested.
- If you would not make a public comment or repost with commentary, answer `No public comment.`

## Questionnaire answers

- Answer every required question.
- For single-choice and multiple-choice questions, use the provided option IDs.
- For Likert questions, provide a whole number within the stated range.
- When a rationale is requested, briefly explain the main reason for your choice.
- Keep your answers internally consistent with how you would actually react to the interaction.

Most questions ask about your own reaction. If a question explicitly asks what you expect the broader public reaction to be, give your personal expectation of that outcome; do not substitute that prediction for your own views in the other questions.
