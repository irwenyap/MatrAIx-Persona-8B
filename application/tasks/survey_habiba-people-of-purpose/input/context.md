# Scenario Context

## Humans of POSB

**Humans of POSB** is a first-person, human-interest storytelling series that spotlights the people behind DBS and POSB beyond their job titles and functions.

The series focuses on employees' personal journeys, values, challenges, motivations, and lived experiences. Stories are told in the employees' own voices and are intended to feel personal, conversational, and relatable rather than like formal corporate communications.

The post below features **Habiba Begum Hajamohidin**, a Collections Officer at DBS/POSB.

---

# Featured Employee

## Habiba Begum Hajamohidin

Habiba works as a **Collections Officer** and has been in Collections Recovery for eight years.

Her role involves helping customers and businesses manage outstanding debts. In describing her work, Habiba emphasizes listening to customers, understanding the wider circumstances affecting them, and helping them find a way forward during difficult periods.

She also participates in Singapore's **National Day Parade (NDP)** as part of the DBS/POSB marching contingent.

The post describes this as her fourth National Day Parade.

---

# Post Caption

> “Boom! Boom! Boom!
>
> The rhythmic beat of drums. The roar of the crowd. The one moment of euphoria when months of hard work shows up in our perfectly synchronised march at the National Day Parade.
>
> Standing on the main stage in my POSB uniform, I am unbelievably honoured to be part of our nation’s birthday celebration. And that’s why I keep coming back.
>
> This year marks my fourth National Day Parade.
>
> In all honesty, when I first signed up in 2023, it was mostly because I wanted to make new friends. At the time, I was also going through a separation and adjusting to a new chapter in my life.
>
> Looking back, I ended up gaining far more than a few new friends. The friendships I formed, the support from fellow marchers and the sense of purpose that came with every rehearsal gave me something positive to focus on.
>
> 'Habiba, let’s go for dinner after rehearsal! It’s more fun when we are all together,’ a colleague suggested one day.” She soon became a close friend.
>
> One dinner turned into weekly meals and long walks outside of rehearsal days.
>
> Week by week, I found myself moving forward again.
>
> But I took away more than friendships from these experiences. Preparing for the NDP requires patience, discipline and the belief that progress comes one step at a time.
>
> These are values I hold close in my role in Collections Recovery, where I help customers and businesses manage their outstanding debts.
>
> I’ve been in this role for eight years. Many of the customers I speak to are navigating difficult periods in their lives. Financial challenges are often just one part of a bigger story. Having gone through my own struggles, I understand how overwhelming it can feel when life suddenly takes an unexpected turn.
>
> That's why I try to listen first, understand their circumstances and work with them to find a way forward. It feels like yesterday when this event became such a core part of my time at DBS.
>
> As I march alongside colleagues on Singapore's birthday, I'm reminded that the spirit of what I do every day isn't so different. Whether it's supporting a customer through a difficult period or moving in step with my fellow marchers, both are about helping people move forward – one step at a time.
>
> Happy National Day, Singapore”
>
> — Habiba Begum Hajamohidin, Collections Officer  
> #HumansofPOSB

# Themes Present in the Post

The post combines several parts of Habiba's personal and professional life:

- participating in Singapore's National Day Parade;
- representing POSB as part of a larger DBS/POSB employee contingent;
- developing friendships and social support through repeated rehearsals;
- describing a difficult personal period following a separation;
- connecting the discipline and persistence required for NDP preparation with her work;
- helping customers and businesses facing debt and financial difficulty;
- emphasizing listening, empathy, and helping people move forward;
- expressing pride in participating alongside colleagues in Singapore's National Day celebrations.

The post presents these themes together as a first-person account of Habiba's experience as both a DBS/POSB employee and an NDP participant.
